Cerveau Studio

Publishing tool for the TikTok account @ton.cerveau.ment0

Cerveau Studio is a personal content tool used by the creator of the TikTok account @ton.cerveau.ment0, a French-language channel explaining psychology and neuroscience (cognitive biases, memory, relationships, famous experiments).

The tool prepares short educational videos (voice-over, subtitles, licensed stock footage from Pexels) and publishes them to the creator's own TikTok account through the official TikTok Content Posting API, after the creator has authorized it with TikTok Login Kit. Every video is labelled as AI-generated content.

The tool is not offered to the public and only publishes to the account of the creator who authorized it.
